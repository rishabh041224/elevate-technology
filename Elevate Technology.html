How we work

Four phases, each with something you can inspect.

No black boxes. At the end of every phase you get a document, a demo or a dashboard, and a clear decision about what comes next.

Phase 1 of 4Weeks 1 to 2

Discover

We interview your teams, read your architecture and measure what runs today. You get an honest picture of risk, cost and opportunity.

  • Current-state assessment
  • Risk and cost baseline
  • Prioritised roadmap
Phase 2 of 4Weeks 3 to 4

Architect

We design the target system, choose the tools and write down the trade-offs, so every decision has a reason you can read later.

  • Solution architecture
  • Security and compliance plan
  • Fixed-scope estimate
Phase 3 of 4Fortnightly releases

Build

Small, tested releases every two weeks. You see working software early and can change direction while it is still cheap.

Operate
